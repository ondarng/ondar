Traditional Storefronts vs. Ondar

The Old, Chaotic Way

Customers DM you asking "is this available?". You reply with pictures, copy-paste your bank details, wait for credit alerts, manually write down delivery addresses, and lose track of inventory. It is slow, unprofessional, and eats up your entire day.

The Ondar Way

You send a single Link-in-Bio storefront link. Customers browse your live catalog, select their sizes, fill their delivery details, and checkout. The order details are instantly formatted, paid for, and waiting on your dashboard.

Ondar's Core Feature Suite

Instant Storefront

Your own custom site under `ondar.shop/yourstore`. Updates stock instantly when you sell items.

Direct Checkout

Accept payments directly online via Paystack card or bank transfer. Clean order logs are generated instantly.

WhatsApp Checkout

Perfect for negotiation. Compiles the cart items, totals, and address, then sends it to your WhatsApp in one click.

Customer Memory CRM

Ondar remembers everyone who buys from you. Search customers by name, see their address, phone, and IG handles instantly.

Digital Receipt Download

Need to send proof of payment? Click "Receipt" and Ondar automatically generates a beautiful PNG receipt you can send over.

1-Click Dispatch Copy

Direct Checkout orders have a copy button that copies the customer's name, phone, item, and address formatted for dispatch riders.
